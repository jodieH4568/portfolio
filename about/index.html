About Jodie

Scientific mind.
Creative instinct.

I take a scientific approach to marketing, with a sprinkle of creativity, to help B2B businesses generate new leads.

A person, not an agency

I start by understanding your past data to help predict what your market will respond to.

Then I use those insights to appeal to your ideal buyers.

My background in Biomedical Science shaped the way I think: ask questions, understand the evidence and connect the dots.

I bring analytical thinking to the strategy and a creative perspective to the execution. This might mean, on one day, making an engineering concept easier to understand for a blog article, and the next, using your data to guide future marketing channel choices and budget optimisation.

B2B doesn’t mean boring. I believe B2B can be both professional and engaging. Understanding your audience is key to knowing how to communicate complex products and services in new, exciting ways.
