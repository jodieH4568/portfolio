Artist rebrand & EP project.

01 / The challenge

A singer-songwriter was looking for a new way to rebrand and capture attention for an EP project she was releasing in the soul-pop genre. Her previous brand identity did not match the new music, and she wanted to attract new opportunities and listeners.

02 / The action

I started by listening to the new music and the artist’s influences. I also identified personality traits she felt were reflected in the new work and how she wanted to come across. There was a desire to be emotional, relatable and playful, yet classic.

She took heavy inspiration from Adele, Duffy, Lily Allen and Amy Winehouse, so I analysed these artists from a branding perspective, looking at colours, fashion and personality.

This helped me develop a colour palette, typography and fashion style that would suit the artist, using a signature white headband to create a recognisable look to be worn at all photo shoots, events and performances.

I organised new photo shoots and videos with photographers and videographers who I knew could capture this new image and edit the photos in a classic, retro way to bring out the emotional and classic elements of the new brand.

03 / The result

The new music received multiple radio plays, secured a major UK festival slot and drove monthly Spotify listeners to their highest level in two years.
