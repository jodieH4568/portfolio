Engineering social media management.

01 / The challenge

A pharmaceutical engineering company expanding its UK presence wanted to grow its social media following in line with its competitors. The business had 330 followers and lacked a consistent online brand identity.

02 / The action

Following an analysis of the brand, its customers and competitors, I developed a social media strategy focused on three main content pillars: expert and informative, building trust, and service awareness.

The expert and informative posts focused on showcasing helpful insights around validation engineering and the problems customers commonly faced, alongside solutions.

The building trust posts focused on how the business maintained quality behind the scenes, alongside testimonial posts.

The service awareness posts focused on the services the business offered and the problems that each service solved.

The strategy followed a 70%, 20%, 10% ratio, with the highest weighting towards expert and informative posts, as these were most likely to gain followers.

I also noticed early on that carousels and posts with pictures of people consistently outperformed posts that did not feature people, so these were some of the common formats we used.

I maintained a weekly posting cadence, allowing the page to stay front of mind for potential buyers.

03 / The result

Following this strategy, the page’s following grew organically from 330 to 1,000+ followers, with 80% of followers matching the ideal buyer profile.
