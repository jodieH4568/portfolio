Marketing material design.

01 / The challenge

A UK engineering company needed its first marketing materials to distribute to its customer base, to strengthen relationships with existing customers and build relationships with new customers.

02 / The action

I set out to design the company’s first brochure, incorporating a laser-cut navigation bar for ease of navigation.

Prior to making the guide, I mapped out the ideal buyers, service pages and call to action. I got clear on the brand colours, incorporating a fresh and professional colour palette which complemented the navy blue already in the logo. I identified fonts that fitted the brand’s trustworthy and professional image, before drafting the guide in Canva.

I worked with key stakeholders in the business, including the company CEO, to further refine the vision and make changes to the guide.

I liaised with a number of printing houses to ensure the best finish was achieved for the best price.

03 / The result

The final guide was signed off by all stakeholders and distributed to over 70 contacts within the first two months. It received positive feedback from existing customers and generated new leads for the business.

This process was replicated 12 months later to produce a digital white paper for online distribution.
