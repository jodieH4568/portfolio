SEO & website redesign.

01 / The challenge

A small engineering company wanted to expand its presence in the UK market and acquire more customers. The current website was generating no inbound leads, and the business was reliant on leads acquired through the CEO’s own contacts.

02 / The action

I conducted a full analysis of the business’s current services and desired service offering, current competitor websites, potential SEO keywords, and brand image.

I then mapped these into a structure centred around a homepage, nine core service pages, a blog section and a contact page.

For each of these pages, I took inspiration from the authoritative, detailed and trustworthy image the business wanted to portray.

I identified the best keywords for each service category and mapped them to the pages that would eventually become the website.

I then wrote the full copy and selected suitable pictures. I created sketches of the site and how I wanted it to function before passing the copy and sketches to a WordPress web developer.

Knowing Google’s core ranking factors, I stressed the importance of the site being quick and responsive and having a good user experience.

03 / The result

The resulting site impressed the management team, but what was most telling was the growth in total website traffic from 33 to 1,466 visits per month — an increase of 4,342% in 24 months.

The number of leads grew steadily from zero to six per month through the website in three years, which was extraordinary for such a niche business.
