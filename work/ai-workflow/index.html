AI workflow & data aggregation.

01 / The challenge

A UK small business wanted to create AI workflows to move its SEO in-house to a small team. The business had a limited budget to aggregate systems and needed to work with existing platforms. The big challenge was having all the decision-making data in one place, enabling decisions to be made about the priority of SEO tasks.

02 / The action

I started by brainstorming all of the data sources that needed to be aggregated to guide the prioritisation of tasks, and listing all of the deliverables of the previous agency. The data sources included Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Semrush and Screaming Frog. The deliverables included technical audits, keyword analysis, keyword mapping, backlink analysis, rank tracking and more.

I decided that these data should feed into an SEO roadmap that updated monthly, containing all of the outstanding tasks that needed to be completed on the site, alongside a priority ranking.

I set up automations on each of the key data sources using a combination of Claude and Microsoft Power Automate to pull the reports and upload them to a shared OneDrive file.

I then set up 14 skills on Claude to replace the tasks and deliverables of the previous agency, making sure that each skill delivered the required output. I tested each of these skills multiple times to further optimise and refine them.

I then created the master skill, the SEO roadmap, which brought all 14 previous skills together in one place, using a tiered system to prioritise outstanding SEO tasks.

Throughout all stages of the build, I reviewed the outputs of each skill to ensure it was producing the desired outcome.

03 / The result

The SEO roadmap system was implemented, enabling a £1,000 per month cost saving for the business compared with using the agency.
